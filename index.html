(01) — The house

We make few things, slowly. Each vessel is thrown from lake clay, dried for three weeks in the fog, and fired once in wood — so the flame, not the potter, signs it.

Chapter 01 — Clay

Dug, sieved,left to rest.

The clay comes from one bank on the Galičica slope. It ages in the cellar for a winter before it ever meets the wheel, which is why our walls can be thrown thin without slumping.

Chapter 02 — Fire

Thirty hoursof pine and ash.

The kiln is stoked every seven minutes, day and night. Ash settles on the shoulders of each pot and melts into a glaze nobody planned — the reason no two pieces match.

Chapter 03 — Glaze

Celadon,the colour of fog.

Our celadon is iron and wood ash, nothing more. It goes on grey and comes out of the fire the colour of the lake at six in the morning.
